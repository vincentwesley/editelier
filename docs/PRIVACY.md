# Editelier privacy policy

Effective 2026-11-25. Applies to the Editelier app for Windows by Vin's Atelier.

## Short version
Editelier collects no data. Your videos, photos, audio and projects stay on your device.

## What the app does with your files
- It reads the media you choose to import and writes the projects and exports you choose to save.
- It keeps preview copies (proxies), thumbnails and waveforms in a local cache on your device so editing is smooth. You can clear the cache in Settings or by uninstalling.
- Your original files are never changed.

## What the app does not do
- No account, no sign-in.
- No telemetry, analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking.
- No uploads. Nothing you edit leaves your device unless you send it somewhere yourself.

## Network
The app makes no network connections on its own. The only exception is a link you choose to click (for example "Get HEVC support" or a help page), which opens in your web browser.

## On-device AI
Auto captions and background removal run entirely on your device. The models are bundled with the app. Your audio and images are never sent anywhere. If larger optional models are ever offered as downloads, the download will only start when you ask for it, and this policy will be updated before that ships.

## Children
The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes
Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new effective date.
