# Editelier privacy policy

Effective 2026-11-28. Applies to the Editelier app for Windows by Vin's Atelier, from version 0.1.0.

## Short version
Editelier collects no data. Your videos, photos, audio and projects stay on your device.

## What the app does with your files
- It reads the media you choose to import and writes the projects and exports you choose to save.
- It keeps preview copies (proxies), thumbnails and waveforms in a local cache on your device so editing is smooth. The cache is in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\dev.vwesley.editelier\cache` (Store installs keep it in the app's own package folder, which uninstalling removes); you can delete it at any time. A clear-cache button in Settings is planned.
- Your original files are never changed.

## What the app does not do
- No account, no sign-in.
- No telemetry, analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking.
- No uploads. Nothing you edit leaves your device unless you send it somewhere yourself.

## Network
The app makes no network connections on its own. The only exception is a link you choose to click (for example in About), which opens in your web browser; the app only opens a short list of its own and its licensors' pages that way.

## On-device AI
Version 0.1.0 contains no AI features or models. Auto captions and background removal are planned and will run entirely on your device: your audio and images will never be sent anywhere. If optional models are ever offered as downloads, the download will only start when you ask for it, and this policy will be updated before that ships.

## Children
The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes
Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new effective date.
