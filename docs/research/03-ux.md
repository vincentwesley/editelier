# 03 - UX research: Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, CapCut (desktop) and photo editors

Date: 2026-11-25. Scope: Editelier, a free Windows video + photo editor. Audience: beginners and creators making YouTube / TikTok / Instagram content on a mid-range laptop, with depth for pros.

**Clean-room note.** This document describes workflows, behaviours and conventions only. Editelier copies no visuals, icons, layouts, colour schemes, names of proprietary features or other trade dress. Keyboard shortcut *conventions* (single keys mapped to common actions) are industry practice; optional keymaps reproduce only key-to-action mappings, never assets.

**Source caveat.** reddit.com and pcmag.com refuse automated fetching from this research tool, so Reddit sentiment is cited through press coverage that quotes those threads (for example [Piunikaweb on r/CapCut](https://piunikaweb.com/2026/01/27/capcut-pro-price-increase-100-dollars-outage/)), and the review layer uses TechRadar, Creative Bloq, Tom's Guide, Capterra, G2, Trustpilot, vendor docs and vendor forums instead. Vendor-written comparison pages (capcut.com/resource, filmora.wondershare.com, movavi.com) are marked as biased where used. Re-check Reddit by hand before a final product decision.

## 1. What users praise

### Premiere Pro
- **Flexible, customisable interface and AI-assisted tools.** TechRadar's 2025 review praises the flexible interface and the new AI features ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/reviews/adobe-premiere-pro-2022)).
- **Ecosystem integration** with After Effects and Photoshop is the most cited strength in user reviews ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/233456/Adobe-Premiere-Pro/reviews/)).
- **Text-based editing** (cut video by deleting words in a transcript) "completely transformed" a documentary editor's process ([Frame.io](https://blog.frame.io/2023/10/16/premiere-pro-text-based-editing/)); bulk filler-word removal is built in ([Adobe help](https://helpx.adobe.com/premiere-pro/using/text-based-editing.html)).
- **Auto Reframe** (16:9 to 9:16 / 1:1 / 4:5) produced reframed versions in under ten seconds on a laptop ([Streaming Media](https://www.streamingmediaglobal.com/Articles/Editorial/Featured-Articles/Review-Adobe-Premiere-Pro-Auto-Reframe-135118.aspx)) and looked more natural than Resolve's equivalent, ~4 s vs ~19 s for a 30 s 4K clip ([Elements.tv](https://elements.tv/blog/comparing-the-auto-reframe-in-davinci-resolve-and-premiere-pro/)).
- **Customisable layouts**: switchers say Premiere's interface is more customisable, while Resolve locks you into preset layouts ([PremiumBeat](https://www.premiumbeat.com/blog/one-year-after-giving-up-premiere-pro-for-davinci-resolve/)).

### DaVinci Resolve
- **Free and very capable**: cost is the top reason people switch ([Creative Bloq](https://www.creativebloq.com/entertainment/video-editing-software/i-finally-switched-from-adobe-to-davinci-resolve-heres-how-it-went), [PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2023/04/18/why-video-editors-are-switching-to-davinci-resolve-in-droves)). Studio is a one-time $295 perpetual licence ([Miracamp](https://www.miracamp.com/learn/davinci-resolve/davinci-resolve-pricing)).
- **All-in-one** (edit, VFX, audio, colour in one app) was a main draw ([Fstoppers](https://fstoppers.com/review/goodbye-adobe-premiere-hello-da-vinci-resolve-466649)).
- **GPU-driven performance** was "the biggest selling point" for one switcher ([Fstoppers](https://fstoppers.com/review/goodbye-adobe-premiere-hello-da-vinci-resolve-466649)); users report Resolve handles phone/DJI footage better than Premiere ([Adobe community](https://community.adobe.com/questions-729/premiere-pro-playback-stuttering-with-vivo-x300-ultra-footage-mac-mini-m4-davinci-resolve-works-correctly-1626331)).
- **Stability vs. cheaper rivals**: Trustpilot reviewers moved from Filmora and Pinnacle because of crashes and found Resolve more stable ([Trustpilot, Blackmagic](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/blackmagicdesign.com?page=3), [Trustpilot, Pinnacle](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/www.pinnaclesys.com)).
- **Caption accuracy** better than Final Cut's ([Larry Jordan](https://larryjordan.com/articles/comparing-captioning-in-final-cut-pro-11-premiere-pro-2025-resolve-20/)).
- **Cut page** for fast assembly under tight deadlines ([Mixing Light](https://mixinglight.com/color-tutorial/overview-resolve-16-cut-page), [PremiumBeat](https://www.premiumbeat.com/blog/davinci-resolve-16-cut-page-review/)).

### CapCut (desktop)
- **Easy and powerful for free**: trimming, splitting and speed ramps take "a few taps and drags"; auto captions are "highly accurate, even with whispered speech" and easy to edit ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/pro/software-services/capcut-review), mobile app).
- **Templates and shortcuts** speed up production ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/pro/software-services/capcut-review)).
- **Desktop adds a fuller timeline, keyframe graph editor and mobile cloud sync** ([Atomi Systems](https://atomisystems.com/screencasting/capcut-pc-review-2026-is-free-video-editing-really-worth-your-time/); [Filmora, competitor](https://filmora.wondershare.com/video-editor-review/capcut-for-pc.html)).
- **Seen as the low-cost, low-learning-curve alternative** to Premiere ([Filmora, competitor](https://filmora.wondershare.com/video-editor-review/capcut-for-pc.html)), though not powerful enough for corporate pro work ([Passionfruit](https://passionfru.it/?p=104982)).

## 3. Workflows to match

| Workflow | Industry behaviour | Editelier recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| **Magnetic vs tracks** | Magnetic timeline closes gaps and keeps connected clips in sync automatically; a "position" mode suspends it ([Apple FCP guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/final-cut-pro/verb8fcfc133/12.0/mac/15.6)). Track editors need manual positioning; magnetic takes adjustment for track users ([Filmora](https://filmora.wondershare.com/advanced-video-editing/final-cut-pro-magnetic-timeline.html)). | **Hybrid**: a main storyline track that is magnetic by default (gaps close, attached B-roll/titles/music follow their anchor) plus free tracks above/below. One toggle ("Magnetic main track") for pros. |
| **Ripple by default?** | Premiere's Q/W ripple-trim to playhead and text edits ripple ([Adobe shortcuts](https://helpx.adobe.com/au/premiere-pro/using/default-keyboard-shortcuts-cc.chromeless.html), [Adobe TBE](https://helpx.adobe.com/premiere-pro/using/text-based-editing.html)). | Ripple delete on the main track by default; Shift+Delete lifts (leaves gap). Never ripple other tracks out of sync. |
| **J/K/L shuttle** | J reverse, K stop, L forward, repeat to speed up ([Adobe shortcuts](https://helpx.adobe.com/au/premiere-pro/using/default-keyboard-shortcuts-cc.chromeless.html)); same in Resolve ([Storyblocks](https://www.storyblocks.com/resources/blog/davinci-resolve-keyboard-shortcuts)). | Ship as-is. Universal. |

| **Blade** | B blade tool, A selection; Ctrl+B or Ctrl+\ split at playhead depending on version ([Pixflow](https://pixflow.net/blog/davinci-resolve-keyboard-shortcuts/), [OBSBOT](https://www.obsbot.com/blog/video-editing/davinci-resolve-keyboard-shortcuts)). | S = split at playhead (one key, no tool mode), B = blade tool, Ctrl+K = split all tracks. |
| **Snapping** | Standard toggle (S in Premiere/N in Resolve). | On by default, N toggles, hold Alt while dragging to bypass temporarily. |
| **Drag-in from media bin** | Universal. | **Templates** | CapCut's ready-made templates speed production ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/pro/software-services/capcut-review)) but get paywalled ([Sikayetvar](https://www.sikayetvar.com/en/capcut-us/capcut-locked-everything-behind-pronow-we-cant-use-basic-features)). | Free, local, editable templates built from normal timeline items (no locked blobs); user can save own. |
| **Aspect switch 16:9 / 9:16** | Auto Reframe works best when the 16:9 edit is finished first, then derived ([Streaming Media](https://www.streamingmediaglobal.com/Articles/Editorial/Featured-Articles/Review-Adobe-Premiere-Pro-Auto-Reframe-135118.aspx)); unwanted fast camera moves in Resolve's version ([Elements.tv](https://elements.tv/blog/comparing-the-auto-reframe-in-davinci-resolve-and-premiere-pro/)). | Project canvas switcher (16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:5) at top of preview. "Make vertical copy" duplicates the timeline with subject-tracked reframe, smoothed, with a "static" option and per-clip manual override. |
| **Export presets** | Premiere offers a YouTube destination preset ([Adobe](https://adobe.com/creativecloud/video/hub/features/best-premiere-pro-export-settings-youtube)) but bitrate/scaling options confuse. ## 4. Pain points to beat, ranked by impact (beginners + creators on a mid laptop)

Impact = how many of our users hit it x how badly it blocks a finished video.

1. **Laggy 4K/HEVC playback on laptops** - blocks editing itself; seen in Premiere and Resolve free ([Adobe community](https://community.adobe.com/bug-reports-728/anyone-else-getting-playback-lag-in-premiere-pro-25-3-1331766), [BMD forum](https://forum.blackmagicdesign.com/viewtopic.php?p=798182)). Fix: hardware decode in the free product + automatic proxies.
2. **Paywall at export / surprise Pro features** - user loses the work they did ([Trustpilot](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/capcut.com)). Fix: everything free; no watermark; no premium-tagged assets.
3. **Crashes and lost work** ([Capterra Premiere](https://www.capterra.com/p/233456/Adobe-Premiere-Pro/reviews/), [Capterra Resolve](https://www.capterra.com/p/209733/DaVinci-Resolve/reviews/)). Fix: continuous journaled save, crash-safe recovery, plain project files.
4. **VFR phone footage audio drift** - the default camera of our audience ([Adobe FAQ](https://community.adobe.com/t5/premiere-pro/faq-variable-frame-rate-vfr-video/td-p/10348229)). Fix: auto-conform on import.
5. **Learning curve / where do I start** ([Creative Bloq](https://www.creativebloq.com/reviews/davinci-resolve-19)). Fix: one workspace, progressive disclosure, guided empty states.
6. **Captions: slow, gated or inaccurate** ([Larry Jordan](https://larryjordan.com/articles/comparing-captioning-in-final-cut-pro-11-premiere-pro-2025-resolve-20/), [CapCut help](https://www.capcut.com/help/auto-captions-in-capcut)). Fix: free local STT, confidence highlighting, fast edit.
7. **Export settings confusion** ([Adobe ref](https://helpx.adobe.com/media-encoder/desktop/encoding-and-exporting/export-settings-reference.html)). Fix: destination cards.
8. **Vertical/horizontal reformatting** ([Elements.tv](https://elements.tv/blog/comparing-the-auto-reframe-in-davinci-resolve-and-premiere-pro/), [Packt](https://subscription.packtpub.com/book/business-and-other/9781801075251/15/ch15lvl1sec13/smart-reframe-on-the-cut-page)). Fix: canvas switch + vertical copy, free.
9. **Subscription cost and cancellation traps** ([DOJ](https://www.justice.gov/usao-ndca/pr/united-states-files-complaint-against-adobe-and-two-adobe-executives-alleged-0)). Fix: free, no account.
10. **Content-rights / privacy distrust** ([Digital Camera World](https://www.digitalcameraworld.com/tech/social-media/capcuts-new-terms-of-service-are-angering-the-internet-and-so-confusing-that-video-editors-are-asking-chatgpt-to-decipher-them)). Fix: offline-first, no account, no telemetry by default, plain-English licence.
11. **Colour grading intimidation** ([Pixflow](https://pixflow.net/blog/davinci-resolve-color-grading-for-beginners/)). Fix: sliders + looks first, layered "advanced" stack later.
12. **Silent failures** (AI, autosave) ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/reviews/adobe-premiere-pro-2022)). Fix: every background job shows status and errors in plain language.
13. **UI churn forcing relearning** ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/233456/Adobe-Premiere-Pro/reviews/)). Fix: stable layout, changelog for moved controls.

## 5. Keyboard shortcut maps (proposal)

Conventions only. Defaults chosen so a beginner can edit with a handful of single keys and pros find standard muscle memory (J/K/L, I/O, Q/W). The Premiere-style and Resolve-style maps are optional presets that remap the same Editelier actions to the key conventions documented publicly by each vendor/community ([Adobe default shortcuts](https://helpx.adobe.com/au/premiere-pro/using/default-keyboard-shortcuts-cc.chromeless.html), [Blackmagic Resolve 15 shortcuts PDF](https://documents.blackmagicdesign.com/UserManuals/DaVinci-Resolve-15-Keyboard-Shortcuts.pdf), [Pixflow Resolve cheat sheet](https://pixflow.net/blog/davinci-resolve-keyboard-shortcuts/)). Verify against current versions before shipping; Resolve's split key varies by version ([Pixflow](https://pixflow.net/blog/davinci-resolve-keyboard-shortcuts/)).

| Action | Editelier default | Premiere-style | Resolve-style |
|---|---|---|---|
| Play / pause | Space | Space | Space |
| Shuttle reverse / stop / forward | J / K / L | J / K / L | J / K / L |
| Step frame back / forward | Left / Right | Left / Right | Left / Right |
| Jump to previous / next edit | Up / Down | Up / Down | Up / Down |
| Mark in / out | I / O | I / O | I / O |
| Clear in / out | Alt+X | Ctrl+Shift+X | Alt+X |
| Split at playhead (selected or all if none) | S | Ctrl+K | Ctrl+B |
| Split all tracks at playhead | Ctrl+K | Ctrl+Shift+K | Ctrl+\ |
| Select tool | V | V | A |
| Blade tool | B | C | B |
| Trim tool | T | B (ripple) / N (rolling) | T |
| Ripple-trim start to playhead | Q | Q | Ctrl+Shift+[ |
| Ripple-trim end to playhead | W | W | Ctrl+Shift+] |

## 6. Onboarding, empty states, undo and errors

**Onboarding / empty states**
- Empty states are "teachable moments" that build confidence and get users started on key tasks ([NN/g video](https://www.nngroup.com/videos/empty-states-in-application-design-guidelines/), [NN/g article](https://nngroup.com/articles/empty-state-interface-design)); they should drive an action, not only explain ([Smashing Magazine](https://smashingmagazine.com/2017/02/user-onboarding-empty-states-mobile-apps)).
- Proposed: launch screen asks one question ("What are you making?" YouTube / Short / Reel / Photo / Carousel) which sets canvas and export preset; an optional "I'm coming from Premiere / Resolve / CapCut" sets the keymap. Empty timeline shows a large drop zone ("Drop clips here") with three inline hints (split S, delete, captions). Empty bin shows Import + "drag from Explorer". No modal tours; inline coach marks dismiss forever with an X.
- Hide multi-page complexity: Resolve's split into Cut/Edit/Color/Fusion/Fairlight confuses beginners about where to start ([TourBox](https://www.tourboxtech.com/en/news/davinci-resolve-pages-explained.html)). Use one workspace with an inspector that grows (Basic / Advanced tabs).

**Undo / save**
- Lost work after crashes is a top complaint in both pro apps ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/209733/DaVinci-Resolve/reviews/), [Adobe community](https://community.adobe.com/t5/premiere-pro-discussions/premiere-pro-cc2018-v12-1-1-crash-work-lost/m-p/9872721)); autosave settings silently resetting erodes trust ([PremiumBeat](https://www.premiumbeat.com/blog/resolve-14s-auto-save-functions/)).
- Proposed: every edit journaled to disk immediately (no save button needed, Ctrl+S still works); unlimited undo persisted with the project; visible History list with named steps and thumbnail; snapshot versions every N minutes kept with the project folder (not a hidden database); "Restore previous version" in File menu; on relaunch after crash, reopen exactly where the user was with a one-line banner.

**Errors**
- AI features failing silently is a cited con ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/reviews/adobe-premiere-pro-2022)); media-offline and codec errors confuse (VFR, HEVC flavours) ([Puget Systems](https://www.pugetsystems.com/labs/articles/What-H-264-and-H-265-Hardware-Decoding-is-Supported-in-DaVinci-Resolve-Studio-2122/)).
- Proposed: plain-language errors with one fix button ("This clip moved. Find it" / "This file uses a format your GPU can't play smoothly. Make a preview copy"); background jobs (captions, proxies, export) in one status tray with progress, cancel and retry; export never fails silently, and a failed export keeps the settings and offers "Try safe mode" (software encode). Non-destructive by design: originals never modified.

## 7. Photo side: Lightroom / Snapseed-style editing and carousels

- **Beginners prefer approachable, minimal tool layouts** (Snapseed) while Lightroom wins on precision ([TechWiser](https://techwiser.com/lightroom-vs-snapseed/), [Mike53 blog](https://mike53onthemove.blogspot.com/2025/10/snapseed-vs-lightroom-mobile-which.html)). Lightroom Mobile paywalls healing, selective masking and geometry ([TechWiser](https://techwiser.com/lightroom-vs-snapseed/)).
- **Looks first, tools second**: Snapseed 3.0 reorganised into Looks / Faves / Tools with pinnable favourite tools ([9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2025/06/12/snapseed-3-0-update-iphone/), [PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2025/06/13/snapseed-updated-with-film-inspired-filters-and-redesigned-edit-experience)); swipe-to-pick-parameter gesture retained ([PopSci](https://www.popsci.com/diy/iphone-photo-editor-snapseed-update/)). Redesigns that move tools anger long-time users ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/apps/google-completely-remade-its-snapseed-ios-photo-editor-222003395.html)).
- **One-click AI masks** (subject, sky, background) select in under a second and are the beginner path to local edits ([Adobe help](https://helpx.adobe.com/lightroom-cc/web/edit-photos/apply-masks/mask-with-ai.html), [Fstoppers](https://fstoppers.com/lightroom/how-use-lightrooms-new-ai-masking-better-landscape-edits-701478)); masks must stay editable afterwards (feather/edge) ([Fstoppers](https://fstoppers.com/artificial-intelligence/lightroom-classic-155-finally-lets-fix-ai-masks-after-creating-them-904005)).
- **Gaps in mobile tools**: no desktop app, no batch editing in Snapseed ([Wondershare](https://repairit.wondershare.com/ai-photo/snapseed-photo-editing-app.html)) - Editelier's desktop batch "copy edits to all" is a differentiator.
- **Carousels**: 1080x1350 (4:5) is the tallest shape allowed and all slides share one shape ([Superprofile](https://superprofile.bio/guides/instagram-carousel-size)); seamless panoramas = canvas of N x 1080 wide split into equal panels, exact widths or seams drift ([Superprofile](https://superprofile.bio/guides/instagram-carousel-size), [PHLEARN](https://phlearn.com/tutorial/seamless-panoramas-instagram-photoshop/)); grid view trims 4:5 so keep subjects centred ([Superprofile](https://superprofile.bio/guides/instagram-carousel-size)); users currently rely on separate splitter tools ([TimeToPost](https://timetopost.co/tools/carousel-splitter/)).
- **Proposed photo UX**: Looks strip (free, local) above a short slider list (Light, Colour, Detail, Crop); AI masks one click; healing brush free; before/after hold-to-compare (backslash); copy/paste edits and sync across selected photos; Carousel mode = a horizontal canvas with slide guides, set ratio once, drag photos/text across seams, export numbered 1080-wide slides plus a grid-crop preview.

## Top 15 design principles for Editelier

1. **Free means free**: no watermark, no paywalled export resolution, no "premium" badges on assets. Nothing the user adds can block export.
2. **Smooth on a mid laptop first**: hardware decode for H.264/HEVC in the free product; automatic background proxies; real-time playback is a release gate.
3. **Never lose work**: journaled autosave, persisted unlimited undo, visible history and versions, crash-reopen-in-place.
4. **Phone footage just works**: detect and conform VFR silently, keep audio locked.
5. **One workspace, progressive depth**: Basic/Advanced inspector, no page-hopping; pro tools are revealed, never removed.
6. **Destination-first**: pick YouTube / Short / Reel / Carousel at start and at export; canvas, safe zones and presets follow.
7. **Magnetic main track, free tracks around it**: beginners can't make gaps or desync; pros can switch magnetism off.
8. **Industry muscle memory**: J/K/L, I/O, Q/W, B, M, Space work out of the box; Premiere-style and Resolve-style keymaps one click away.
9. **Captions are one button**: local, offline, editable as text, low-confidence words flagged, styled, burn-in or SRT.
10. **Every action visible, keys optional**: all shortcuts have a menu/button path; `?` shows a searchable cheat sheet.
11. **Plain-language errors with a fix button**; no silent failures in AI, saving or export.
12. **Non-destructive and private**: originals untouched, offline-first, no account, no telemetry by default, user owns their content outright.
13. **Looks before knobs** (video colour and photo): presets and simple sliders first, layered advanced grading behind it.
14. **Stable UI**: don't move controls between releases without a migration hint; users hate relearning.
15. **Teach in place**: empty states and inline hints drive the next action; no forced tours, everything dismissible.

## 10-line summary

1. Users praise Premiere for flexible UI, ecosystem and text-based editing; Resolve for being free, all-in-one and GPU-fast; CapCut for ease, one-click accurate captions and templates.
2. Biggest frustrations: Premiere's subscription (FTC/DOJ suit, $75M settlement), crashes and 4K laptop lag; Resolve's learning curve, node colour page and free-version GPU/10-bit limits; CapCut's export-time paywall, ~$100 price hike and content-rights distrust.
3. Phone VFR footage causes audio drift in both Premiere and Resolve; both need manual CFR transcoding today, so auto-conform on import is an easy win.
4. Proxy setup is multi-step in Premiere; Editelier should benchmark playback and build preview copies automatically.
5. Workflow target: hybrid timeline (magnetic main track + free tracks), ripple delete by default, J/K/L, I/O, Q/W, S split, snapping on, drag from bin or Explorer.
6. Captions: one button, local STT, transcript-as-editor, confidence highlights; reframing: canvas switch plus "make vertical copy" with smoothed subject tracking.
7. Ranked pain points: laptop playback, export paywalls, lost work, VFR drift, learning curve, captions, export confusion, reframing, subscriptions, privacy.
8. Shortcut map: Editelier default plus Premiere-style and Resolve-style presets (key conventions only), all actions also reachable via UI and a `?` cheat sheet.
9. Onboarding: "What are you making?" sets canvas/export, "coming from..." sets keymap, action-driving empty states, journaled autosave and plain-language errors with fix buttons.
10. Photo: looks first, few sliders, one-click AI masks, free healing, batch copy edits, and a carousel mode exporting exact 1080x1350 seamless slides; Reddit/PCMag were unfetchable, so re-check Reddit sentiment by hand.
